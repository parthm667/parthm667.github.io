# Yaru icons

These unmodified PNG icons are from the Ubuntu Yaru project by the Yaru
contributors, derived from the Suru icon theme by Sam Hewitt.

Source: https://github.com/ubuntu/yaru/tree/master/icons/Yaru/256x256

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ (full text in LICENSE).

Local filenames map to upstream files:

- folder.png: places/folder.png
- text.png: mimetypes/text-x-generic.png
- pdf.png: mimetypes/application-pdf.png
- image.png: mimetypes/image-x-generic.png
- link.png: mimetypes/text-html.png
