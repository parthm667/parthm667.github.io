The Wild Goose Chase

At 7:42 on Tuesday morning, a goose walked into this classroom, picked up a fishbowl containing one (1) gambling goldfish named Bubbles, and walked back out. It left a trail across this webpage.

Follow it. Ten pieces of evidence. Twenty points each.

How this works

Each piece of evidence is hidden somewhere in this page's HTML, CSS, or JavaScript. Find it, and it gives you a code. Type the code into the box at the bottom of the screen.

A correct code does two things: it banks your 20 points, and it unlocks the next piece of evidence. The goose's trail only appears one step at a time. There is no skipping ahead. Evidence 07 does not exist on this page, in any readable form, until you have solved 06.

  • Every code is lowercase with dashes. Capitals, spaces and stray punctuation are all forgiven.
  • Every answer is hidden in this page's own HTML, CSS or JavaScript. Nothing depends on knowing something from outside.
  • You will live in DevTools. Right-click → Inspect is where you start, not where you finish: the later evidence hides in corners of it you haven't opened yet.
  • Read the hint before you hunt. Each one tells you what kind of hiding place you're looking at.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back.
  • Submit all ten codes on the form when you're done. Partial credit for partial chases.
